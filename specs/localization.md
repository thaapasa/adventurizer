# Localization

The app texts are available in English and Finnish. The language of a book is a
separate setting of the [book](books.md).

- [ ] At the start, the user picks the language of the app: English or Finnish.
- [ ] All app texts, such as buttons, menus and messages, show in the picked
      language.
- [x] When the user creates or reads a book, the app texts are in the language
      of the book.
